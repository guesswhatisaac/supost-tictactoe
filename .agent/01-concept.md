# Concept & Vision

<!-- STATUS: INITIALIZED -->

## Vision

A narrator-led comedy built around a complete, polished Tic-Tac-Toe game.
The player is themselves, playing a browser game. The narrator reacts to their
choices with dry, understated humor and an awareness of the game's mechanics.

## User-selected direction

- **Main experience:** narrator-led comedy with Tic-Tac-Toe as the playable core.
- **Player role:** themselves playing a browser game, with no assigned fictional identity.
- **Humor:** dry and understated, using The Stanley Parable as a reference while keeping the writing original.
- **Scope:** one polished scene with variation across replays. A branching narrative with multiple endings is outside the current scope.

## Pillars

- Player choices give the narrator material, while all valid moves remain available.
- The board, turn, result, and reset remain clear and usable regardless of narration.
- Replay variation adds personality within one scene rather than expanding the project into multiple scenes or endings.
- Keep the single-sitting scope focused on a complete game and polished presentation.
